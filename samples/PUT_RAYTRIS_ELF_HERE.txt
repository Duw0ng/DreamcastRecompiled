DreamcastRecomp 0.0.27 - real homebrew probes

Copy the raytris.elf from the supplied KallistiOS/raylib corpus here as:

    samples\raytris.elf

Then run:

    run_homebrew_raytris_pixeldata.bat
    run_homebrew_raytris_color.bat
    run_homebrew_raytris_main_probe.bat

The project does not redistribute raytris.elf.

Or run all three in sequence:

    run_homebrew_raytris_suite.bat
